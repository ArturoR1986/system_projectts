# Release Notes and Change Log

## Personal Strategy and Alignment v0.3.0

**Release date:** 2026-09-07  
**Change type:** Minor-version architectural integration  
**Status:** Stable candidate; static validation complete after build; live validation pending  
**Persona:** Elias — The Purpose Strategist v1.0.0  

## Release objective

Integrate strategic operating stages, stronger focus and action conversion, governed pattern intelligence, capability compounding, next-horizon behavior, and the Elias persona without violating the clean-start architecture or 8,000-character Project Instructions limit.

## Added

- Two-layer router separating operating stage from functional module.
- Operating stages: Stabilize, Explore, Decide, Execute, Reflect.
- Cross-cutting Focus Card and three-active-objective limit.
- Opportunity states: replace, activate, queue, observe, park, decline.
- Planning-to-exposure guard and research stopping discipline.
- Purpose archaeology and anti-vision methods.
- Capability ledger with demonstrated, transferable, developing, and unsupported states.
- Strategic-lineage trace from intention through evidence and review.
- Elias — The Purpose Strategist as a separate employee/persona layer.
- Persona calibration rules and collision tests.
- Next-horizon process after milestone completion.
- Expanded record templates and 26 behavioral acceptance cases.
- Installation procedure with conversation-only, checkpoint, and connected-storage modes.
- Static validation report recording core length, compression, privacy, terminology, and integrity checks.

## Changed

- The former career-coach framing is formally expanded into Personal Strategy and Alignment.
- Router logic now identifies timing/state before selecting a lead function.
- Action conversion is required only for action-oriented cycles, preserving journal listening, intentional rest, recovery, and safety pauses.
- Priority recommendations require user confirmation before consequential reprioritization.
- New opportunities cannot silently displace established focus.
- Reflection patterns require specific observations, exceptions, alternatives, and user review.
- Purpose is treated as a revisable direction tested through evidence and experience.
- Human-dynamics and power awareness is explicitly defensive and agency-preserving.
- Persona behavior is compactly embedded in Project Instructions while the full specification remains a source document.

## Preserved from v0.2

- Clean start with no seeded user identity, history, destination, or tools.
- Separation of reusable sources, blank templates, and private working records.
- Journal-only and just-listen controls.
- Original-account versus interpretation separation.
- Research source quality and stopping conditions.
- Explicit authorization for submissions and external commitments.
- Prepared/sent/completed/verified handoff distinctions.
- Honest limitations around storage, memory, automation, and external tools.
- User authority and revisable goals.

## Excluded

- Personal user history or preloaded goals.
- Direct imitation of public teachers or authors.
- Runtime dependence on tarot, symbolic readings, or motivational claims.
- Manipulative power tactics, manufactured financial pressure, guaranteed outcomes, and universal bottleneck claims.
- A database, calendar, email connection, background worker, or automation not actually installed by the environment.

## Known limitations

- Prompt compliance cannot guarantee persistent or isolated storage.
- Reference sources may be static in a ChatGPT Project; updating records may require a downloadable checkpoint or connected editable storage.
- Static tests validate file structure and wording, not live model behavior.
- Research and execution depend on tools available in the running environment.
- Persona calibration requires representative conversations.

## Change-control procedure

1. Identify the proposed change and reason.
2. Classify it as patch, minor, or major.
3. Identify core, router, persona, record, test, and privacy impact.
4. Modify the smallest appropriate layer.
5. Update terminology and dependent files.
6. Add or revise tests for behavioral changes.
7. Re-run core length, clean-start, JSON, link, and archive checks.
8. Record actual pilot evidence before promoting release status.

## Promotion rule

Promote from Stable Candidate to Pilot-Ready only after the required smoke tests pass in the installed environment. Promote to Stable only after the five-session pilot produces no blocking failure and the review confirms useful routing, focus, agency, evidence discipline, and persona calibration.
