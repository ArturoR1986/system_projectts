## Section 2 — PeakLogic Positioning Essentials

PeakLogic is positioned as **practical operational support for construction, roofing, and field-service businesses** — not AI hype.

### Core Promise

> *PeakLogic helps [company type] stop losing [time / money / energy / flow] to [specific operational problem] by turning [messy input] into [clean useful output].*

### Positioning Principles

1. **Sell Painkillers, Not Vitamins** — Solve problems customers already feel: wasted office time, incomplete field reports, scattered photos, slow customer updates.
2. **Field-First, Office-Useful** — Respect how field crews actually work. Do not force roofers to become office workers.
3. **AI-Assisted, Not Worker-Replacing** — Position as a system that sharpens the team, not replaces it. Avoid language about automating everything blindly.
4. **Practical Before Impressive** — Use customer language: *faster reports, cleaner records, easier handoffs, better customer updates, less rework, searchable job history.*
5. **Start Simple and High-Value** — The best first offers are narrow, painful, and easy to understand.
